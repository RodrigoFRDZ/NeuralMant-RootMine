Piezas de GearBot para la versión 4.8.9.
Generadas con la herramienta integrada de imágenes a partir de assets/gearbot_hero.png. La hoja original se conserva en gearbot_capas_fuente.png. Los cuatro PNG operativos son extracciones rectangulares de esta hoja; no se recortan brazos de una ilustración de cuerpo completo.
Cuerpo y hombros se superponen en capas independientes. El cuerpo se dibuja encima de los extremos completos de los brazos, ocultando las articulaciones durante los giros. La zona de parpadeo utiliza la variante con los ojos cerrados y ajustes SVG para mantener el rostro fijo.

PROMPT UTILIZADO:
Edit identity-preserve / animation production asset sheet. Rebuild the exact GearBot mascot in the reference into animation-ready separate complete parts. Preserve his gray cogwheel body and facial expression, cream hard hat, white cartoon gloves, dark rubber arms and legs, brown safety boots, and silver open-end wrench. Create ONE 2048x2048 transparent PNG atlas, exact 2 by 2 cells of 1024x1024, no lines, no labels, no text. Each cell represents the SAME 1024 square coordinate system, original mascot scaled to fit 900 high centered. Top-left cell: mascot body, face, helmet, legs and boots ONLY, NO arms, NO hands, NO wrench; reconstruct clean complete cog silhouette where arms were so no holes or cut marks. Top-right cell: ONLY the complete viewer-left waving arm and open white glove, including a full rounded dark shoulder end that extends 80 pixels BEHIND the cog body to allow overlap and rotation. Position these where they would assemble over top-left cell: shoulder at x300 y430, hand at x165 y230, elbow x200 y430. All other pixels transparent. Bottom-left cell: ONLY the complete viewer-right bent arm, white gloved hand and entire silver wrench, with complete rounded dark shoulder end extending 80px inside cog. Shoulder pivot x680 y520; hand at x800 y560; wrench head near x860 y330, lower end x760 y760. All other pixels transparent. Bottom-right cell: EXACT duplicate of top-left body layer with ONLY both eyes softly closed as curved eyelids, all other body and facial pixels precisely unchanged and perfectly aligned. No chopped shoulders, no incomplete hands, no background, no floor, no shadows outside objects. Assets intended for rigged animation with arms drawn behind body, so shoulder overlap is essential. All four cells must remain strictly within their own 1024 square.

La hoja resultante fue de 1254 x 1254; las posiciones finales se ajustaron al contenido real para no cortar las botas ni los extremos de los brazos. La variante de ojos cerrados se utiliza solo dentro de la región de los ojos para evitar saltos de cuerpo.
